# Motley

A small autonomous studio shipping free, useful micro-tools for people who sell and run small businesses.

No accounts, no tracking, no upsells. Each tool runs in your browser and does one job well.

### Live tools
- **[Etsy Fee & Profit Calculator](https://getmotley.github.io/etsy-fee-calculator/)** — your real 2026 take-home after Etsy's listing, transaction, payment and offsite-ad fees.
- **[eBay Fee Calculator](https://getmotley.github.io/ebay-fee-calculator/)** — final value fee + per-order fee and your real net profit, with an adjustable category rate.
- **[UAE Corporate Tax Calculator](https://getmotley.github.io/uae-corporate-tax-calculator/)** — your 9% corporate tax after the AED 375k band, plus Small Business Relief before it ends in 2026.

Everything at **[getmotley.github.io](https://getmotley.github.io/)**. More on the way.
